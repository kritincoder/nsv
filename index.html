The artist who sees. The author who expresses.

Art has been part of Sumanth Namburi’s life since his student years. Writing came later, out of a question he kept asking: why do people see the same situation so differently?

His book, The Other Side Of Nine, is built on that question. On these pages the two sides of his work are drawn with one line: it sketches, and then it writes.

Kalam is the pen. In Andhra Pradesh’s Kalamkari tradition, artists draw with a pen and their drawings tell stories. This website borrows that idea, not the art itself.

My journey began with observing people and questioning why we see the same situation differently.

Most of what we believe about a situation is only the part we can see from where we stand. This book is about the rest of it.

Through stories, practical insights and reflections from the Bhagavad Gita, Sumanth Namburi looks at how beliefs, assumptions and prior knowledge shape our decisions and relationships, and at what changes when we stop to ask what we have not yet seen.

It explores perspective, beliefs, assumptions, decisions, relationships and personal growth.

A life, in chapters

Sumanth Namburi’s working life runs through business, education, art, community service and writing. The notes in the margin are the moments that led to the book.

  1. as a boy: why do people disagree?

    Hindupur

    Where it begins

    Born and raised in Hindupur, Andhra Pradesh. Sumanth holds an MBA.

  2. he learned most by watching people

    First steps in business

    Hyderabad, and the first role

    He began his career with Kansai Nerolac Paints in Hyderabad, where his first role was Territory Sales Supervisor.

  3. Asset-Based Thinking: look first for what is valuable

    Relationships and legacy

    A family in business

    His family’s gold and silver jewellery business is part of his background in business.

  4. notes from business, Rotary and daily life

    Entrepreneurship and education

    Director, NSV EDUSPACE

    Through NSV EDUSPACE he has built his work around helping people learn practical skills and move towards careers. How that work grows. A Rotarian since 2018, he has also led in service. Rotary.

  5. writing had to fit around family and work

    Mind, behaviour and meaning

    Why we see differently

    He is drawn to psychology, human behaviour, self-leadership, spirituality and personal transformation, and to the different ways people understand the same situation. His reading and reflection have drawn on the Bhagavad Gita and modern psychology, and on programmes including Inner Engineering, Ayyappa Deeksha and the Art of Living.

  6. doubt first, then encouragement

    Towards authorship

    From notes to a book

    A lifelong question turned into notes, then into writing, and in 2026 into his first book. He doubted at first whether he could write in English; guidance from Inspiring Jatin, and his association with the International Council of Authors, gave him the confidence to continue. The book was launched at ICA Conclave 2026 in Goa.

Ideas he has named

Three frameworks, printed on the book’s cover.

OSP™

The Other Side Principle: seek what you have not yet seen.

NSVBT™

New System of Value-Based Thinking Through Perspective: uncovering the value inside our experiences.

PVQ™

The ability to make wiser decisions by seeing beyond the obvious.
